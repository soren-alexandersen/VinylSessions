Catalog smart. Listen more.

AI-powered cataloging meets curated listening. Vinyl Sessions helps you rediscover your record collection through intelligent features like AI-generated listening sessions, musician connection graphs, and daily album highlights.

Enjoy your vinyl like never before.

"Vinyl Sessions exists because spreadsheets and auction sites drained the joy out of collecting. I wanted an app that was engaging, looked great, helped me understand my collection and listen to it more often.

Vinyls are not just meant for shelving. They're meant to be played. As a supplement to liner notes, this app allows me to explore the network of musicians across my collection, and reminds me why I fell in love with vinyl in the first place."

Create an inspiring listening session

Select genres, choose duration, and let AI curate a track-by-track listening journey. Perfect for parties, quiet evenings, or rediscovering your collection.

Explore Musical Connections

Every musician, every credit, every collaboration—mapped across your collection. Discover who played on what, and follow the threads through decades of music.

Powerful Settings

Customize your experience with visual themes, display options, and collection management tools. Export your data, adjust your view, and make Vinyl Sessions truly yours.

Seamless Discogs Integration

Import your entire Discogs collection instantly with full API integration. Artwork, release details, condition grades, and genres—all synced automatically. Your digital catalog meets your physical collection.

Understand Your Collection

Get AI-powered insights about your collection. See patterns in your listening habits, discover underplayed gems, and understand the story your records tell about your musical journey.

AI-Generated Album Stories

Every album has a story—the recording sessions, the cultural moment, the artists behind it. Let AI bring those stories to life, deepening your connection with each record in your collection.
